1.1 [requirements.txt is in UTF-16 LE, needs to be in UTF-8:

Format-Hex .\backend\requirements.txt | Select-Object -First 2

Look at the first bytes: FF FE - UTF-16 LE

In root:

# Путь к файлу
$path = ".\backend\requirements.txt"

# Читаем файл в исходной кодировке UTF-16 LE
$content = Get-Content -Path $path -Raw -Encoding Unicode

# Сохраняем в UTF-8 без BOM
$fullPath = (Resolve-Path $path).Path
$utf8 = New-Object System.Text.UTF8Encoding($false)

[System.IO.File]::WriteAllText($fullPath, $content, $utf8)]
